Heat Flow Equation

The heat flow rate \( \dot{Q} \) is calculated using \( \dot{Q} = \frac{Q}{t} \), where \( Q \) is the total heat and \( t \) is the time in seconds. The average heat flow is given by: \[ \dot{Q} = \frac{Q}{t} \]

Reynolds number

\[ Re = \frac{\rho\,u\,L_{c}}{\mu} = \frac{G\,L_{c}}{\mu} = \frac{u\,L_{c}}{\nu} \]
