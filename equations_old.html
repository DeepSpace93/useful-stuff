Equations

Heat Flow Equation

The heat flow rate \( \dot{Q} \) is calculated using \( \dot{Q} = \frac{Q}{t} \), where \( Q \) is the total heat and \( t \) is the time in seconds. The average heat flow is given by: \[ \dot{Q} = \frac{Q}{t} \]

Dimensionless numbers

Reynolds number

forced convection \[ Re = \frac{\rho\,u\,L_{c}}{\mu} = \frac{G\,L_{c}}{\mu} = \frac{u\,L_{c}}{\nu} \]

Biot number

steady- and unsteady conduction \[ Bi = \frac{h\,Y}{k} \]

Fourier number

unsteady conduction \[ Fo = \frac{a\,t}{Y^2} \]

Graetz number

laminar convection \[ Gz = \frac{G\,Y^2\,c_{p}}{k} \]

Grashof number

natural convection \[ Gr = \frac{g\,\beta\,\Delta T\,Y^{3}}{\nu^{2}} \]

Rayleigh number

natural convection \[ Ra = Gr\,Pr \]

Nusselt number

natural or forced convection, boiling, or condensation \[ Nu = \frac{h\,Y}{k_{f}} \]

Peclet number

forced convection (for small Pr) \[ Pe = \frac{U\,Y}{a} = Re\,Pr \]

Prandtl number

natural or forced convection, boiling, or condensation \[ Pr = \frac{c_{p}\,\mu}{k} = \frac{\nu}{a} \]

Stanton number

forced convection \[ St = \frac{h}{\rho\,U\,c_{p}} = \frac{Nu}{Re\,Pr} \]

Knudsen number

\[ Kn = \frac{\lambda}{L} = \frac{k_{B}\,T}{\sqrt{2}\,\pi\,d^{2}\,p\,L} \]

Mach number

\[ Ma = \frac{u}{c} \]
