Oxidizing acids
  • strong Brønsted acids that act as powerful oxidizing agents, usually containing oxygen in their anionic structure
  • readily accept electrons from metals and other substances, often producing nitrogen oxides, sulfur dioxide, or water instead of hydrogen gas
  • e.g. HNO₃, H₂SO₄, HClO₄
    • UL 94
  • plastics flammability standard released by Underwriters Laboratories of the United States
  • determines the tendency to either extinguish or spread the flame once the specimen has been ignited
